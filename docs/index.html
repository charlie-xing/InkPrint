Turn your e-ink reader into a wireless printer.

InkPrint is an Android app for BOOX and other e-ink readers. Print from any Mac, iPhone, Windows, Linux or Android device — every page lands as a PDF on your reader, ready to read on e-ink.

Printing, minus the printer

Every app on every OS already knows how to print. InkPrint simply makes your e-reader the destination.

1

Install & start

Install InkPrint on your BOOX or Android e-ink reader, tap Start printer service. That's the whole setup.

2

Hit Print anywhere

On your Mac, iPhone, Windows, Linux or Android device, press Ctrl+P / ⌘P. InkPrint shows up like any printer — zero-config AirPrint on Apple devices.

3

Read it on e-ink

Seconds later the document is a PDF in /Documents/InkPrint/ on your reader. Read, annotate and sign it with your stylus. Zero paper touched.

Made for my daughter's eyes

InkPrint was inspired by my daughter, Kiki. To protect her eyesight, she does all her reading on an e-ink reader. One day she said: if we care about the environment, we should waste less paper — so why not "print" straight to the e-reader instead?

She was right. It turned out to be simple and practical: every computer and phone already knows how to print, so nothing had to change. Pick the printer, press Print, and the document lands on the reader instead of on paper.

Thank you, Kiki. 💚

Under the hood

For the curious: it's a real print server, not a hack.

  • IPP 2.0 print server written in Rust (inkprint-core crate), compiled to Android via cargo-ndk, bridged to Kotlin with UniFFI.
  • AirPrint-compatible — advertises over mDNS/Bonjour (_ipp._tcp + AirPrint subtype), so macOS and iOS discover it with zero configuration.
  • Windows and Linux connect over standard IPP Everywhere / manual IPP setup.
  • 100% local: no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no tracking. Your documents never leave your Wi-Fi.
