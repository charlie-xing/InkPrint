InkPrint turns your BOOX (or any Android e-ink reader) into a wireless printer. Documents printed from any computer or phone on your network are saved as PDF files on the reader instead of on paper.

Every computer and phone already knows how to print, so nothing else needs to change: pick InkPrint as the printer, press Print, and the document lands on your reader.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>IPP printer, compatible with AirPrint: macOS, iOS and iPadOS find it automatically</li>
<li>Works with Windows, Linux (CUPS) and Android's built-in print service</li>
<li>Every job is saved as a PDF, ideal for e-ink reading</li>
<li>Choose your own save folder, and browse, rename, move, share or delete files in the app</li>
<li>Can also be reached over Tailscale or a VPN by adding the printer by IP</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
InkPrint collects nothing: no accounts, no analytics, no servers, no outbound connections. Everything stays on your device and your local network.

<b>How to use</b>
1. Open InkPrint and tap Start Printer Service
2. Add the printer on your computer or phone (the app shows the steps for each platform)
3. Print — the PDF appears in the app and in your save folder
